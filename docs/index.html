Sudoku Logic

Classic number puzzle for Android

Sudoku Logic is an offline sudoku app. Five difficulties, pencil marks, and hints or mistake checks that can be topped up by watching a short video.

  • No account, no sign-in, no server
  • Nothing to sync — your game stays on your phone
  • English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian
