Privacy policy

Sudoku Logic · effective DATE_PLACEHOLDER

Sudoku Logic is an offline puzzle game. It has no user accounts and no server of its own: nothing you type or play is sent to us. This page explains what the app stores on your device and what our advertising partner collects.

What we store

Your current game, your hint and check balances, and your preferences are saved on your device only. They are never uploaded, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You only see an ad when you choose to watch one for an extra hint or check. Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier and device informationServing and measuring ads, fraud prevention
IP address and coarse locationRegional ad delivery and legal compliance
Ad interactions (impressions, completions)Reporting and reward validation

See Google's own explanations: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

Consent

Where the law requires it (EEA, UK and some US states), a Google-certified consent form is shown before any ad loads. Ad requests are currently made as non-personalized, so ad content is not tailored using your profile.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can decline to watch ads at any time; the game remains fully playable. To remove stored data, uninstall the app. To reset your advertising identifier, use your device's privacy settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
